## What is BORDS?

BORDS is a work OS — a shared digital workspace where teams plan, collaborate, and build knowledge together. Every project gets its own board: an infinite canvas where plans, tasks, guides, and team conversations all live in one place.

Built by AXECORE Labs Inc.

---

## Core Concepts

### Boards
A board is an infinite canvas. Teams use one per project, client, or workflow. Inside a board you can place:
- **Sticky Notes** — quick ideas with customizable colours, expandable into full notes
- **Checklists** — tasks with due dates, assignees, and email reminders
- **Kanban Boards** — columns with draggable task cards, priorities, and statuses
- **Text Blocks** — rich text for documentation and guides
- **Media** — images and files uploaded to the canvas
- **Tables** — structured data
- **Drawings** — freehand sketches, arrows, highlights, shapes
- **Connections** — visual lines linking items to map relationships
- **Reminders** — time-based notifications so nothing slips

### Company Brain
As your team works on boards, BORDS builds a shared memory of your organisation's knowledge — decisions, processes, how things work. Anyone on the team can later ask questions and get answers grounded in real work history.

### BORDS AI
AI built directly into every board. You control which boards the AI knows about. Ask questions, draft plans, write guides — right inside the board, not in a separate chat. Useful answers can be saved back to a board.

## Real-Time Collaboration

- **Live cursors** — see teammates working in real time
- **Multi-user editing** — simultaneous edits with conflict-free syncing (YJS CRDTs)
- **Offline-first** — boards work without internet; changes sync on reconnect with no data loss
- **Voice & video** — start a call directly inside a board via LiveKit
- **Threaded comments** — discuss changes in context on any board
- **Board permissions** — Owner, Editor, Viewer roles

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there a free plan?**
Yes — free forever with unlimited boards and 3 collaborators per board.

**Do you charge per user/seat?**
No. BORDS charges per workspace, not per person.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes. Every board works without internet. Changes sync when you reconnect — no data loss.

**Can I use BORDS for personal projects?**
Yes. The personal workspace is fully private and free.

**What AI model does BORDS use?**
BORDS AI is powered by a model integrated into the platform. You control which boards the AI has context over.

**How is BORDS different from Notion or Miro?**
BORDS combines a visual infinite canvas (like Miro) with structured workflows and AI (like Notion), but is built around real-time team collaboration and a shared knowledge base — not just documents or whiteboards.

**What is the Company Brain?**
It's the knowledge layer that builds automatically as your team works. Past decisions, guides, and context become searchable and usable by AI — so new team members can get up to speed and nobody has to ask "how do we do this again?"

**Is my data private?**
Yes. For team plans, boards are private by default. Enterprise customers get a fully dedicated environment.

**How do I contact support or book a demo?**
Visit bords.app and use the "Book a Demo" button, or reply to any confirmation email — it goes straight to the team.
